# Cutting troubleshooting

Start from a preset for a similar material. Change one setting at a time in the right
panel, try a small pattern on a fresh spot, and compare the result. Use Save to update
the preset, or Save As to keep a new one. Until saved, changes stay in this session;
selecting a material loads its saved settings.

## The cut is too shallow

Check that the blade is clean, seated fully and locked. Increase force gradually.
If the blade cannot reach through the material, increase its depth one step by hand.
For thick or dense material, an extra pass may help. More force cannot make an
underexposed blade reach deeper.

## The cut is too deep or cuts the backing

Reduce force. If it still digs into the backing at low force, reduce blade depth.
Use only enough blade and force to separate the material. For vinyl and iron-on,
the carrier should stay intact; for unbacked material avoid cutting into the mat.

## Tearing, rough edges or distorted corners

Try a lower speed, then less force. Excess blade exposure can snag, so check the
blade depth too. Inspect the blade for damage or trapped scraps. For thicker
material, try lighter force with an additional pass rather than one aggressive pass.

## Material lifts, shifts or bunches during cutting

Secure the material flat on a clean mat suitable for it, and check the blade is
clean and properly installed. Reduce speed; excessive force or blade exposure may
drag the material. More force will not fix poor adhesion. If vinyl lifts from its
liner, also check the material and liner are sound.

## A shape does not separate at its starting point

Try a small increase in overcut. CutCraft's overcut extends a closed contour past
its starting point; it does not extend every corner. Avoid excessive overcut,
which can leave a visible mark. If corners are still wrong, inspect the artwork
in Overlay and review blade-offset compensation under Advanced.

## Iron-on lettering reads backwards after application

Enable Mirrored before cutting iron-on from the back. Check the material's loading
notes and which side faces the blade. Inspect Cut Preview before sending the job.

## The machine moves but nothing cuts

Check blade installation and exposure, then force. Confirm the intended rows are
ticked under What to cut and that cut lines appear in the preview. A picture may
need tracing; a photo may need preparation in VectorCraft or Illustrator first.

## Remember the physical blade

Blade depth is a dial instruction for the original Cameo: CutCraft cannot turn it.
When changing the value in the panel, change the blade's dial too. A small pattern
helps find settings; fine details and large designs may still need another adjustment.
